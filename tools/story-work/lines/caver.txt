I've noticed the drips speed up just before the countdown. Drip, drip, drip, faster and faster. Then five big drips, and the world changes.

The underground pool has no bottom. I've dropped a stone in and listened for a whole world. Never heard it land.

Every world, there's a new tunnel in the cave I've never explored. I always explore it. It always leads back to here. Every single time.
Some people think the world is built from the top down. I think it's built from the bottom up. Starting down here, in the dark, where it's quiet.
I found a lantern deep in the cave, still lit. No footprints anywhere near it. Just the lantern, on a ledge, waiting.
The bats in the cave all fly out together just before the countdown. A big rush of wings. Then the world changes, and they all fly back in.
When the world shows mercy, the drips stop for a moment. Total silence. Then they start again, a little slower. Like the world is giving itself time.

The enemies never come into the cave. Too dark, maybe. Or maybe they know there's something down here that's older than them.

Stalagmites grow up from the floor toward the stalactites. In a few worlds, they'll touch. I want to be here when they do. Something might happen.
I've heard voices down here. Not scary ones. Soft, far away. Like people in another cave, in another world, wondering the same things as me.
There's a rumor that the deepest cave goes all the way through the world, and comes out the other side. Into what, nobody knows.

Fennick says the rain is pieces of old worlds. Well, all the rain sinks down here eventually. So this cave is full of old worlds. Drip by drip.
