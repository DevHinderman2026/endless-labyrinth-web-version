Reading your stats.

Stats belong to the copy of the game they were made in. On an iPhone or iPad, the game on your Home Screen keeps its own scores, separate from Safari's: to see the Home Screen copy's stats, open this page from inside it (on the game's start screen, choose Back to the home page, then Game Stats). A different device or browser starts from nothing. To carry your play from one to another, use Score Backups.
