Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

This policy covers the website, the game it plays, and the Windows version of the game. It is short because there is little to say: neither version collects anything about you.

1. The game collects no personal data

Endless Labyrinth, on the website and on Windows, has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no purchases. It does not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and it does not track you.

2. On the website, your play stays in your browser

Your high scores, stats, settings and event runs are kept in your own browser's storage, on your own device. The game's sound packs are cached on your device so they are not downloaded twice. None of this is sent anywhere. The Game Stats and Events pages read it from your browser alone.

3. On the website, nothing is sent during play or during backups

The game sends nothing about you during play. The only requests it makes are ordinary ones to GitHub, to read the list of events and to learn the time. A backup file goes only where you choose to save it; the site never receives it.

4. GitHub, which hosts the site

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the game reads its events from GitHub. Like any web host, GitHub sees the ordinary details of a visit, such as your IP address and the pages your browser asks for, under GitHub's General Privacy Statement. We do not receive those details.

5. The share buttons

The footer of each page has buttons for sharing this site on Facebook, X (Twitter) and other services. Those services' own code loads only when you tap one of their buttons, never with the page, so they see only visitors who choose to share, and they see them under their own privacy policies, not this one. Sharing is optional.

6. The Windows version

The Windows version keeps your high scores, Event High Scores and settings in its players folder, on your own computer, scrambled and sealed. They are never uploaded. The game plays offline. When it has an internet connection, it contacts only the following, and sends nothing about you:

  • GitHub, to read the list of events and the event files, and to learn the time from GitHub's answer, so that events start and end when they should;
  • GitHub, to see whether a newer version of the game has been released, so that the main menu can tell you; nothing is downloaded;
  • GitHub, to download the game's latest release, only if a sound in its sounds folder is missing or damaged, so that the sound can be put back; the download is deleted when that is done;
  • NV Access, the makers of NVDA, to download NVDA's controller library, only if it is missing from the game's folder, for Braille display support.

As with any download, GitHub and NV Access see the ordinary details of those requests, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies; GitHub's is linked in section 4.

7. Children

Anyone of any age may play either version, and nothing is collected from anyone, children included.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Questions go to the contact address in the footer of every page.
