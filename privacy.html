Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") is issued by Russell Hinderman, trading as DevHinderman2026 (the "Developer"), and describes the treatment of information in connection with Endless Labyrinth. It covers the website, the game it plays, and the Windows version of the game, and it forms part of the Terms of Use. Expressions defined in the Terms of Use have the same meanings in this Policy. The Policy is brief, because the Game collects no personal data: its purpose is to set out precisely what is stored on your own device and what, if anything, is transmitted.

1. No collection of personal data

Endless Labyrinth, on the Website and on Windows, collects no personal data. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat facility and no purchases. It does not request your name, your email address or any other information about you, it does not employ cookies or analytics for the purpose of tracking, and it does not profile or track you in any manner. The Developer receives no information about any person from the Game.

2. Data stored in your browser

On the Website, your play stays in your browser. Your high scores, statistics, settings and Event records (together, your "Save Data") are held in the local storage of your own browser, on your own device, and the Game's sound packs are cached on your device so that they need not be downloaded again. None of this data is transmitted to the Developer or to any other person. The Game Stats and Events pages read it from your browser alone. You may delete it at any time by clearing your browser's storage for the Website.

3. Transmissions during play and backups

The Web Game sends nothing about you during play. The only requests it makes are ordinary requests to GitHub, to read the list of Events and the Event files, and to the Website itself, to learn the current time. A backup file of your Save Data is written only to the location that you choose; the Website does not receive it.

4. GitHub, the hosting provider

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Game reads its Events from GitHub. In common with every web host, GitHub receives the ordinary technical details of a visit, such as your IP address and the pages that your browser requests, and processes them in accordance with GitHub's General Privacy Statement. Those details are not disclosed to the Developer.

5. Share buttons

The footer of each page contains buttons for sharing the Website on Facebook, X (formerly Twitter) and other third-party services. The code of those services is loaded only when you tap one of their buttons, and never with the page itself, so that those services receive information only about visitors who choose to share, and do so under their own privacy policies rather than this Policy. Sharing is entirely optional.

6. The Windows version

6.1 Data stored on your computer

The Windows Program stores your high scores, Event High Scores and settings in its players folder, on your own computer, in scrambled and sealed form. They are never uploaded. The Program functions offline.

6.2 Transmissions

When the Program has an internet connection, it communicates only with the following services, and transmits no information about you to any of them:

  1. GitHub, to read the list of Events and the Event files, and to obtain the current time from GitHub's response, so that Events begin and end at the correct moment;
  2. GitHub, to determine whether a newer version of the game has been released, so that the main menu may inform you of it; nothing is downloaded by that check;
  3. GitHub, to download the Program's latest release archive, in either of two circumstances only: when you choose Check for Updates and confirm the update by pressing Y, in which case the archive is used to install the update and is then deleted (see section 6.3); or only if a sound in its sounds folder is missing or damaged, in which case the sound is restored from the archive and the archive is then deleted;
  4. NV Access, the developer of the NVDA screen reader, to download NVDA's controller library, only if that library is missing from the Program's folder, for the purpose of Braille display support.

In common with any download, GitHub and NV Access receive the ordinary technical details of those requests, such as your IP address, and process them under their own privacy policies; GitHub's is linked in section 4.

6.3 Updates

From version 2.0.0, the Program is able to update itself, but only when you request it. No update is downloaded or installed without your confirmation, and the Program contains no automatic update facility. An update replaces the Program's own files only; the players folder, containing your Save Data, is not read, transmitted or altered by an update. The Program writes a single line to a file beside itself recording whether the update succeeded, reads that line aloud when it next starts, and then deletes the file. Nothing concerning an update is transmitted to the Developer or to any other person.

7. Children

Anyone of any age may play either version of the Game. The Game collects no information from any person, and therefore collects no information from children. The Developer does not knowingly receive personal data from any child.

8. Changes to this Policy and contact

The Developer may amend this Policy from time to time. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the Policy was last updated. Questions concerning this Policy should be directed to the contact address given in the footer of every page.
