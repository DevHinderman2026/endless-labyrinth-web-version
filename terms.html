Common questions about these Terms

Plain answers to the questions people ask most. The numbered sections above are what count.

Can I record or stream the game on YouTube?

Absolutely! Recording, streaming and posting Endless Labyrinth is completely fine, and encouraged. Audio games are still a niche, and videos are how more people find out that games like this exist. Put the game's name and a link to this site in your description, describe what is happening for your listeners, and have fun.

If YouTube flags your video. The game's sounds come from Pixabay under its Content License, which allows them in videos, but YouTube's automatic Content ID matching sometimes flags a recording by mistake. If that happens:

  1. Dispute the claim, saying the sounds are used under the Pixabay Content License and are credited on the game's Credits page. YouTube explains how at Dispute a Content ID claim.
  2. If the claimant rejects your dispute and stands by the claim, you can appeal the decision.
  3. If the claim blocks your video, YouTube may offer Escalate to Appeal, which skips the dispute and goes straight to an appeal, and gives the claimant seven days to respond instead of thirty. See Appeal a Content ID claim.
  4. Be aware that after an appeal, a claimant who still disagrees can file a formal takedown request, which can put a copyright strike on your channel. Appeal when you are confident; for the game's own sounds, you have good reason to be.

Follow YouTube's Community Guidelines, at YouTube's Community Guidelines, and the rules of any other platform you use. Good things to do: name the game and link to this site in the description, describe what is happening for listeners who cannot see the screen, keep titles and comments respectful, and set the made-for-kids setting correctly when asked. Things not to do: misleading titles or thumbnails, spam or fake giveaways, harassment, hate or bullying, showing cheats or hacks, pretending to be the developer or the official channel, selling the game or charging for it, paid promotion of the game without permission, and re-uploading someone else's video as your own.
