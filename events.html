Events calendar

What is on now, what is coming, and what has ended, with your own best runs.

Events are limited-time challenges, shared with the Windows version: the same signed event files, so what you see here is exactly what the game plays. To play one, open the Events menu inside the game. This page is for reading: it does not start a run.

Dates are shown in your own time zone. What counts as on now is judged by internet time, the same way the game judges it, so changing your device's clock makes no difference. Your runs, medals and best scores come from this browser alone; nothing is sent anywhere.
