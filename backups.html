Score Backups

Keep your scores, stats and settings safe in a file, and bring them back whenever you need them.

Why back up

The game keeps your high scores, your stats, your settings and your event runs in this browser's own storage, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. That storage can be lost: if you clear your browser's data, if Safari removes a website's data after a week without visiting it (playing from the Home Screen prevents that), or if you move to a new device. A backup file keeps everything, and can be restored here or on any other device.

On an iPhone or iPad: the game on your Home Screen keeps its own scores, separate from Safari's. Open this page from the copy whose scores you want: for the Home Screen copy, open the game from your Home Screen, choose Back to the home page on its start screen, then Score Backups. A backup made in Safari and restored in the Home Screen copy (or the other way round) is how you move your scores between the two. Share the backup file, then Save to Files, is the surest way to keep the file.

Back up

The file holds every high score, your stats, your settings and your event runs. It is sealed, so a file that has been changed is refused when it is restored. Keep it somewhere safe, such as Files on an iPhone or iPad, iCloud Drive or Google Drive.

Restore

Choose a backup file. Nothing changes until you decide what to do with it: combine it with the play saved on this device, keeping the better of everything, or replace what is saved here with the backup. You can also cancel.

What combine and replace do

  • Combine keeps the better best score on every board, the larger total for time played and runs, and the better run and medal in every event, so nothing is counted twice. The settings on this device are kept.
  • Replace puts the backup in place of everything saved on this device, settings included.
  • If the game is open in another tab of the same browser, close that tab first, or return it to its main menu, so that it takes in the restored scores when it next saves.
