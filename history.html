Update History

What each version of the web version of Endless Labyrinth brought.

This page lists the web version. For the Windows version, see its changelog on GitHub.

The game updates by itself: the next time you open it, you have the latest version. Only a sound pack that has changed is downloaded again, and your high scores are kept. Events are not listed here: they appear in the Events menu as they open.

6.3.7 (22 November 2026), web only

A temporary fix for the static on iPhone and iPad; beacons that could fall silent after a pause; backgrounds that stay when an exit appears; echoes that ring out when you pause; fairer ammo on the islands; and credits that no longer vanish in Chaos mode.

A bug hunt, from one end of the game to the other.

The static on iPhone and iPad: a temporary fix

  • This is a temporary fix for the static some players hear on iPhone and iPad. It isn't completely solved yet and we're still working on it, but it should be much better than in the last update. It can still happen: if you hear static, pause the game and resume it, and it disappears.
  • What changed: a sound you could not hear, such as a beacon out of earshot, was still being worked on by the phone as if you could, because its loudness only ever crept toward nothing and never reached it. Now a silent sound is truly silent, and the phone can leave it alone. In a test of thirty-two such sounds, the work fell by more than half.
  • Every run now also starts on a fresh sound engine: the first time you touch the main menu after a run, the game makes a new one, so nothing from the last run is carried into the next.
  • A beacon you walk back into earshot of no longer comes in with a tiny click.

Fixed: beacons that stayed silent after a pause

  • Now and then, about once or twice in a hundred pauses, resuming the game could leave some beacons silent for the rest of the level, and Game resumed was not said. In Standard mode that could be the exit itself. It happened when the pause landed in the first hundredth of a second of a beep. It was introduced in 6.3.6 and is fixed; the same fault could happen on leaving Help, or on saying No to returning to the main menu.

Changed: the background stays when the exit appears

  • In Chaos mode's Abyss levels and Find the Voice levels, the background used to fade away when the exit appeared, leaving only the exit's Morse signal. Now the wind of the abyss, or the room's background, plays on until you walk through the exit, and then fades into the next level like any other. The creatures, the cases, the lightning and the guide still go, as before.

Changed: an echo rings out when you pause

  • Pausing while a bell rang in Call of Fate cut the bell and its echo off in the same instant. Now the sound stops and its echo dies away in the room, over about a second, as it would in a real one. When you resume, the sound and its echo come back together, in step. This reaches every held sound with an echo, in every mode and event: bells, exits and beacons.

Changed: ammo on the islands

  • In Island mode, and on Chaos mode's islands, ammo could pile up: every seabed laid out a full supply whatever you already carried, so fifty shots or more by the second or third island was normal. Now a seabed holds what its island needs and a spare, less what you carry: a shot for each enemy, the hits its guardian takes, and, if a guardian waits on the next island, that guardian's hits and a shot for each enemy that can be after you at once there. The spare is two to four shots and half of the need again.
  • Each reinforcement that lands while you are down now leaves one more shot on the seabed. Until now three were allowed for whether they landed or not.
  • There is always at least one small cartridge to find, cartridges still hold two to six shots, and what you leave on the seabed is still there on your next dive.
  • A careful player always has enough. In twenty thousand pretend runs, a player who hits four shots in five had enough ammo for eight islands in 96 runs out of 100; one who hits seven in ten, in 69. Every miss counts now.

Changed in Chaos mode: credits and cartridges stay when the exit appears

  • When a level was won and its exit appeared, a time credit still lying there vanished, even one you were a step away from. Now a credit already out stays, its beacon still sounding, and you can take it for its time: in a battle, on a tower floor and on a road. A cartridge already out in a battle stays too, and its shots still go to your battles' ammo.
  • No new credit or cartridge appears once a level is won, as before, and the clock still waits while the exit is open.

Changed in Chaos mode's worlds: the water slide

  • A water slide could be ridden again and again for thirty seconds each time. Now the first ride in a world adds thirty seconds, and each ride after it five fewer: thirty, twenty-five, twenty, fifteen, then ten for every ride after that. Every slide in the world counts together, and a new world starts again at thirty. The points for a ride are unchanged.

Fixed in events: creatures that listen for you

  • In an event whose creatures listen for you, a creature could settle right beside the thing you had to reach, a key, a door, a treasure, or face it, so that you could only reach it by waking the creature. Now they keep clear of everything you have to reach.
  • High scores are not touched by this update.

6.3.6 (21 November 2026), web only

The exit's beeping now comes from one place, the exit, even after you have turned; and so does every other sound that is heard from a place.

A sound should be where it says it is.

Fixed: the exit's beeping heard to your left or right while you faced it

  • In Standard mode and in Chaos mode's labyrinths, you could turn to face the exit and still hear its beeping to one side, or on a different side from one beep to the next, as if it were reflecting off something. The cause: the game reuses its sound channels, and a browser stops keeping a channel up to date while nothing plays through it, so a reused channel had missed your turns. The next beep then came from where the exit had been, relative to your head, the last time that channel played, and the channels were of different ages, so the beeps disagreed with each other. Measured in the game, a beep you were facing began 3 to 7 decibels to one side. Now a channel is brought up to date the moment it is about to play again, and every beep comes from the exit from its first moment to its last.
  • The same fix reaches every sound heard from a place, in every mode, Ultimate Mode and Chaos mode's worlds included: a wall you walk into, a pickup, a glass case's knock, the elevator's chime, the safe zone characters' voices, an enemy's hit. It also covers a beacon that carries on after a pause, and one you walk out of earshot of and back to.
  • No new sound channels are made for this and none is ever unplugged, so the popping fixed in 6.3.5 stays fixed. High scores are not touched.

6.3.5 (21 November 2026), web only

Every high score starts fresh; the labyrinth beeps and the popping, put back the way they were in 6.3.2; a slower rival in the towers; and The Abyss's creatures stop when you stop again.

Sometimes the best fix is the one from yesterday.

Changed: every high score starts fresh

  • With so much refined over the last few updates, every mode's high score board has been set back to zero for all players, the first time you open 6.3.5, so everyone starts fresh. A backup made before this version does not bring the old scores back. Event High Scores, play time and runs are not affected.

Fixed: the beeps and the popping in the labyrinths

  • In Standard mode and in Chaos mode's labyrinths, the exit's beeping is now heard from the exit's own place from the very start of every beep, as the glass cases' knocking is. Before, each beep began straight ahead of you and was moved to the exit's side only once it had started, so it never seemed to sit in one place. Placed sounds also once again use the sound engine the way they did in 6.3.2, which had no popping. The game no longer makes new sound channels while a free one is there to use; each sound takes the free channel already pointing nearest its place. Only chimes and spoken voices keep 6.3.3's care to start exactly from their own place.

Changed: Tower mode's rival

  • The rival racing you for items is slower at the start, a little over a walking pace instead of well over it, in Tower mode and on Chaos mode's tower floors. It still gets a little faster every tower, so the climb still ends in the end.

Changed: The Abyss's creatures

  • The creatures stop hunting you when you stop again, as before 6.3.4, in The Abyss and in Chaos mode's Abyss levels. In 6.3.4 they kept coming to where they last heard you, and since they heard every step you took, you could not get away from them, walking or running. The harder clock, the rarer extra life and lightning up to twenty seconds apart stay.

6.3.4 (21 November 2026), web only

The Abyss is harder again, and the popping in the labyrinths is gone again.

6.3.3 made The Abyss so friendly that the creatures were practically holding the door for you. They have been spoken to.

Fixed: popping in the labyrinths

  • In Standard mode and in Chaos mode's labyrinths, the sound could pop every second or two while you walked, between the exit's beeps. It came from 6.3.3's fix for the chimes: as you walked, the game kept making new sound channels and unplugging old ones, and unplugging one can be heard as a pop. Nothing is unplugged now. The game reuses the channel already pointing nearest the right way, so chimes and voices still start from their own place.

Changed: The Abyss

  • The clock starts at forty-five seconds instead of sixty, and every case gives ten or twenty seconds, never thirty or sixty. In Chaos mode's Abyss levels, the cases give the chaos clock what they did before.
  • A creature that has heard you keeps going to the spot where it last heard you, even if you stop, and gives up when it finds nothing there. So standing still where it heard you is no longer safe: get away by walking or running out of its hearing, since it is slower than you, or by moving while the lightning covers your steps. The same in Chaos mode's Abyss levels.
  • Lightning is never more than twenty seconds apart again, as before 6.3.3, in The Abyss and in Chaos mode's Abyss levels.
  • The extra life is rarer: one case in twenty instead of one in ten.

6.3.3 (21 November 2026)

Fixes only: chimes and voices start from their own place, the sound is a little quieter and lighter work for a phone, the compass guides you with a beep, chasers no longer pin you against a wall, and The Abyss has easier cases to find, more lightning and extra lives.

No new modes this time, only fixes. The chasers in the battle areas have learned to take turns, which is more than can be said for most people at a sale.

Fixed: chimes and voices start from their own place

  • A sound heard from a place in the world, such as the elevator's chime, the bus's chime or a character's voice, could begin for a moment from wherever the sound before it had been, so a chime on your right started on your left and a voice could sound lopsided. Every sound now starts from its own place, in Ultimate Mode and in every mode heard in 3D.

Fixed: the static on phones

  • The game's limiter, which keeps the sound from going over full volume, added a little loudness of its own, even to quiet sounds, and at the loudest moments that could still go over. That extra loudness is now taken back out, in every mode.
  • Ultimate Mode, and the worlds of Chaos mode, were mixed too loud, so the limiter was working nearly all the time. They are now a little quieter.
  • Every sound heard from a place now takes half the work it did, which helps a phone keep up when a lot is going on at once.

Changed: the compass in Ultimate Mode

  • The compass is now a guide. It says only where it is taking you and how far that is by the walking route, such as Compass on. Safe zone, 23 steps. Then a soft beep comes from the next turn of the route. Turn until the beep is straight ahead of you, where its pitch is highest, and walk to it, turn after turn, until you arrive and it says so, such as You are in the safe zone. Compass off. Inside a safe zone it guides you out, and while a bus is waiting for you it guides you to the bus.
  • It comes on with the world frozen: the enemies stop and the world goes muffled, and a double tap describes the world around you. Your first step lets the world move again, and the guide carries on while you play. The world stays frozen for half a minute at most; after that the compass cannot freeze it again for fifteen seconds, though it still guides you.

Fixed: characters and their gifts

  • In Ultimate Mode and in Chaos mode's worlds, a safe zone's character spoke and handed you its gift while you were still two or three steps away. Now you need to be on its square or the one beside it.

Fixed: chasers pinning you against a wall

  • In Ultimate Mode's battle areas, and in Chaos mode's worlds, a crowd of chasers could corner you against a wall with no way past them. Now at most three of an area's chasers come for you at once. The others wait their turn at least four steps away and never strike; shoot one, and the next one comes.
  • A cartridge that comes back after being taken now goes first to the battle area or island you are in, if that area has none, so you are not left without ammo where the enemies are.

Changed: The Abyss

  • Only the nearest glass case knocks. The other two only hum, more softly, and the knocking moves to another case only when you smash this one or come two steps nearer to another, so lining up a case no longer takes so long.
  • One case in four now holds lightning, instead of one in six, in The Abyss and in Chaos mode's Abyss levels.
  • In The Abyss itself, one case in ten also holds an extra life: you hear the heart, and the voice says it with your lives, such as 10 more seconds. Extra life, 4 lives. There is no limit on lives.
  • Lightning is never more than fifteen seconds apart, instead of twenty.
  • Each strike is one of three thunderclaps. One that sounded dry, not like a real thunderclap, is gone.

Changed: the sound of breaking glass

  • Smashing a glass case, in Ultimate Mode, The Abyss and the events, now sounds like glass breaking. Before, it sounded more like something being crushed. The Windows game will have the same sound from version 4.0.0.

6.3.2 (20 November 2026)

Lightning in The Abyss grows further apart again as you go; and every chime is heard from one point.

Four versions in one day, and this is the one where the lightning goes back to how it was this morning. The creatures stay the way 6.3.1 made them, though: they now keep three polite steps apart, which is more personal space than anyone working on this update got today.

Changed: lightning in The Abyss

  • Lightning strikes every six to ten seconds at first and, every three cases you smash, comes a little less often, never more than twenty seconds apart, as it did before 6.3.1, in The Abyss and in Chaos mode's Abyss levels. With lightning every six to ten seconds the whole way, the clock could climb to four minutes, so the run never got harder. The creatures still keep their distance from one another, as in 6.3.1, and keeping them apart never moves one that isn't hunting you any closer to you.

Fixed: chimes heard from one point

  • A chime, a bell or a ding is now heard from one point: the elevator's, the bus's, the chime when you reach the guide in Find the Voice, and every event's pickups. Some of them were recorded in stereo and, played where you stood, were spread from ear to ear instead. An event's bell or gong that starts or ends it is now heard from one point too, as is any chime or bell an event brings with it. The same in Windows version 4.0.0.

6.3.1 (20 November 2026)

The popping in the labyrinths and the static on the islands of the worlds, both fixed; lightning in The Abyss every six to ten seconds all the way through, with the creatures spread out; and every background now turns with you.

A confession: the popping came from 6.3.0's fix for the static. That is a bit like fixing a squeaky door by taking the door off. The door is back on, and it no longer squeaks. The islands, meanwhile, had simply been turned up far too loud, like a neighbour who discovers a new speaker.

Fixed: popping in the labyrinths

  • In Standard mode, and in Chaos mode's labyrinths, the sound could pop every few seconds, between the exit's beeps. It came from 6.3.0's fix for the static: each finished sound was unplugged from the game's mix a few seconds after it ended, and some devices can be heard to pop whenever that happens. A finished sound's part in the mix is now handed on to the next sound instead, so nothing is unplugged as you play, and nothing piles up either.

Fixed: static on the islands of the worlds

  • In Ultimate Mode and in Chaos mode's worlds, an island, the elevator ride and the seabed were far louder than the rest of the world, the seabed most of all, so the game's own volume limiter was holding everything down the whole time you were there, and a limiter working that hard is heard as crackle. The elevator's hum, which was the loudest thing on the seabed, is now quieter, as are the ride, the island's own sound and the water, so they sit at the level of the rest of the world.

Changed: lightning and the creatures in The Abyss

  • Lightning now strikes every six to ten seconds for the whole run, in The Abyss and in Chaos mode's Abyss levels, instead of coming less often as you smash more cases.
  • The creatures no longer crowd together. Lightning lands further off, fifteen to twenty-five steps away, every creature runs to it, and each makes for its own spot around the strike instead of all piling onto the same one. The creatures always keep about three steps apart from one another, and a creature that wakes does so fifteen to twenty-five steps from you, never right beside another.

Changed: every background turns with you

  • In every mode that turns with you, the background turns too. Four backgrounds were recorded with both their sides the same, so there was nothing to turn and they sounded the same whichever way you faced; three of them are labyrinth backgrounds, so about one Standard level in four seemed not to turn. Each of them is now given a second side of its own as it loads, so it turns like the rest. The same in Windows version 4.0.0.

6.3.0 (20 November 2026)

One health bar for the whole of a Chaos run; time on the Chaos clock for everything you score in a Chaos world; footsteps that match the ground in outdoor safe zones; gaps between wall corners you can walk through in every labyrinth; and the static that could still creep into the sound the longer you played, found and fixed for good.

Just above, it says the static was "found and fixed for good." For good lasted about three hours. See 6.3.1.

Changed: one health bar in Chaos mode

  • Chaos mode now has one health bar for the whole run, starting at 100, in place of separate lives and health. Every level you finish adds 10, with no limit.
  • An enemy reaching you in a battle or on an island no longer ends the run: it costs 25 health and the enemy is gone, and in a battle the crowd groans again. An island's guardian costs 25 too, but if it reaches you while you are out of shots, it takes all your health. Out of air on a seabed, you lose 10 health a second until you reach air. A crash on a road costs 20 health as well as its 30 seconds. A creature catching you in a Find the Voice room or an abyss costs 25.
  • A tower's health potion, or a world's rare full heal, brings you back to 100 if you are below it. A world now starts with the health you have and hands back what is left.
  • When your health first falls to 35 or less, the voice says Health low and how much is left, in every kind of level. Every double tap in Chaos mode tells you your health.

Changed: time in Chaos worlds

  • In a Chaos mode world, everything that scores points now puts time on the Chaos clock as well: 20 seconds for a chaser shot down, 30 for a thrower, 10 for ammo or a potion picked up, and 30 for a chest's points. As with the points, nothing counts inside a safe zone or while the compass is on. A water slide still adds 30 seconds, and the glass cases still hold their time credits.

New: footsteps in the open air

  • In Ultimate Mode's worlds and in Chaos mode's worlds, a safe zone out of doors is now walked on what it sounds like: grass among crickets, cicadas, birds, bees, trees or a forest, at a campfire or in a graveyard; snow in a blizzard or a winter wind; mud in rain, a storm, a marsh or by a river; sand in a desert or by the ocean at night. Everywhere else is still stone. The grass steps are new recordings, in a small sound pack of their own, so only it is downloaded.

Changed: corners in the labyrinths

  • In Standard mode and Chaos mode's labyrinths, where two pieces of wall touch only at their corners, with floor beyond, you now fit through the gap between them when you face it at a slant, as you already could in Ultimate Mode. An opening you hear straight ahead is one you can walk through. The same in Windows version 4.0.0.

Fixed: static in the sound, again

  • The static that could creep onto the edges of sounds the longer you played, in Chaos mode especially, and that vanished while the game was paused, had two more causes. Every sound placed in 3D left a small piece of itself behind in the sound engine when it finished, so a long run built up thousands of them for your device to keep working through. And every sound's loudness, and in the worlds its place, was sent to the sound engine sixty times a second, about eight thousand changes a second in a world; pausing stopped them, which is why the static went away. A finished sound now leaves nothing behind, and a change too small to hear is no longer sent, so a world sends about a fifteenth as many.

6.2.0 (20 November 2026), web only

A new mode made for the web alone, The Abyss, which Chaos mode now visits too; the creature in Find the Voice no longer gives up so soon; and a fix for static that could creep into the sound the longer you played.

Somewhere in The Abyss, a creature is still waiting for the lightning to strike. It will be waiting a while longer once you get to 6.3.2.

New: The Abyss

  • A huge, endless abyss with no walls and no edge, open air where nothing echoes back, and the whole floor is broken glass, so every step you take can be heard. Blind creatures live all through it and hunt by sound: they come for you only while you move. Walk, and the ones nearby creep toward your steps; run, and they charge from farther away; stop, and they go back to wandering the abyss very slowly, never toward you and never within three steps of you.
  • Lightning strikes every few seconds, really loud, and the creatures run toward it. For five seconds after a strike your steps are not heard, so that is the time to move. It comes less often as you go, but never more than twenty seconds apart.
  • Smash as many glass cases as you can before the clock runs out. Each one adds time: usually ten seconds, sometimes twenty or thirty, and very rarely sixty. Smashing glass is loud and brings every creature that hears it charging for a few seconds, unless the lightning has just struck. About one case in six holds lightning of its own, which sends the creatures away instead.
  • The clock starts at sixty seconds and you have three lives. Every three cases, one more creature wakes. Your score is the cases you smash, on a high score board of its own. The help has a section and demos for it, and so does the Help page here.
  • Chaos mode has Abyss levels too: lightning strikes the moment you arrive, you smash a set number of cases, each adding its time to the chaos clock, and then everything else goes silent and only an exit is heard. Abyss levels had three lives of their own for the whole chaos run; from 6.3.0, being caught there costs 25 of the run's one health bar instead.

Changed: Find the Voice's creature

  • Once it hears you on the glass, the creature now charges for at least five seconds, even if you step straight off again, and keeps coming for three seconds after you are back on concrete (it used to stop about a second after). The same in Chaos mode's Find the Voice levels, and in Windows version 4.0.0.

Fixed: static in the sound

  • On some devices, iPhones especially, a slight static could creep onto the edges of every sound the longer you played, clearing for a while after a pause. Every sound that moved, and every turn you made, left the sound engine doing far more work than it needed, for good, until the device could not keep up. Each movement now settles once it is done, so the work stays light however long you play.

6.1.0 (19 November 2026)

The game's voices are now recordings: Bella, the Find the Voice guide and every safe zone character always speak in their own voices, at once, on every device. Windows version 4.0.0 will have the guide's recordings and the same battle and event changes.

Changed: the voices

  • Every line Bella says during a run, every call of the Find the Voice guide and every line of Ultimate Mode's twenty-six safe zone characters is now a recording, made once for the game with the same Kokoro voices, and downloaded with the game's sounds, about 95 megabytes. Nothing is made on your device any more, so the voices no longer fall back to your device's voice, and the characters are always heard in their own voices.
  • A line the game puts together, such as a score, a time or a direction, is said by joining the recordings of its words and numbers. Only words typed into an event made after this version have no recording; your device's own voice says those.
  • There is no wait before the guide speaks: her first call comes as soon as the level has been said.
  • The game no longer downloads anything for its voices from other sites, and the page no longer loads itself a second time when you first open it.

Changed: Battle mode's crowd

  • The crowd's cheer when you shoot an enemy down is a little quieter, and so is its roar when a wave begins, now as loud as a kill's cheer.
  • The crowd no longer groans or gasps when an enemy reaches you: in a battle one hit ends it. Ultimate Mode's battle areas keep their reactions.
  • The stadium crowd's murmur is now heard all through the fight, everywhere in the arena: its recording had a long quiet stretch that sounded like no crowd at all, and it is now played twice, half a loop apart, so one fills the other's quiet.
  • The same in Chaos mode's battles.

Fixed: Ultimate mode's labyrinth areas

  • Where two pieces of wall touched only at their corners, the gap between them stopped you like a wall, even with open floor beyond and the safe zone or a voice heard straight through it. You now fit through such a gap when you face it at a slant. The same in Chaos mode's worlds. The Standard labyrinth is unchanged.

Fixed: Island mode's seabed

  • Since backgrounds began turning with you, the deep water of the seabed had been playing more than twice as loud as it was made to be, loud enough by itself to push the game's sound over the top. With the swimming strokes, the beacons and the elevator on top, the sound could distort, like static, most of all riding the elevator up. The water is back at its intended loudness, as in the Windows game. The same on Chaos mode's islands.

Fixed: in events

  • Call of Fate: "Ring three times" and the like were said as soon as the climb to the next level began. They are now said just before the climb ends, and the level begins right then.
  • Count the Chimes: after a miscount, the next count's chimes rang over the voice telling you what the number was. The next count now waits a couple of seconds.

6.0.3 (19 November 2026)

Battle mode's crowd goes wild when a wave begins, and the beacons are louder over it. Windows version 4.0.0 will have the same.

  • In Battle mode and in Chaos mode's battles, the cartridge and credit beacons are now twice as loud, held to full loudness up close, so you hear them over the arena crowd. Ultimate Mode's battle areas have done this since the crowd came in.
  • When a wave begins, with the war horn after the five, four, three, two, one, the crowd goes wild: a great roar from all round the arena, louder than for any kill, and the crowd's own murmur swells for a few seconds and settles back.

6.0.2 (19 November 2026), web only

Find the Voice gives the guide's own voice time to be ready, opens with the level alone, and follows the Speech setting.

  • Find the Voice gives the guide's voice time: a few of her calls are made as soon as the game has loaded and kept ready, her first call waits a few seconds for her own voice if it is still being made, and a later call waits a moment before your device's voice says it. Until now her first calls nearly always went to your device's voice.
  • Find the Voice now opens with "Level 1." alone; the mode's name was only just heard on the menu.
  • With the Speech setting turned Off, no voice is heard for the guide in Find the Voice: her soft shimmering beacon plays where she stands in place of her voice. (Until now her calls were spoken whatever the setting.)

6.0.1 (19 November 2026), web only

The game's own voices, Bella, the Find the Voice guide and the safe zone characters, are made faster and are heard far more often.

  • The voices are now made with several of your device's processor threads where the browser allows it, which is several times faster than before. The first time you open the game's page after this update, the page loads itself once more, straight away, to switch this on.
  • The game was too quick to hand over to your device's voice when a line was late, and then kept to it for the rest of the visit. It now waits longer before doing so, and after a minute it tries its own voice again.
  • If a device cannot start several threads, the voices are made with one, as before.
  • On the main menu, and in the game's help, every mode now comes before Events: Standard, Battle, Driving, Tower, Island, Find the Voice, Ultimate Mode, Chaos Mode, and then Events and Event High Scores.

6.0.0 (19 November 2026)

A new mode, Find the Voice; a voice of the game's own, Bella, during a run; characters who live in Ultimate Mode's safe zones; two new kinds of level in Chaos; an arena crowd in every battle; sounds placed more precisely; new things for events; less air on the seabed; and many fixes. Windows version 4.0.0 follows later; it brings Find the Voice, 3D sound and turning to the Windows game, and this version's mode rules too, so the two play alike and events stay the same in both. Version 5.2.0 was never released: everything it was to bring is part of 6.0.0, and is listed here.

New: Find the Voice

  • A new mode. A woman's voice, the guide, calls to you from somewhere in a dark room: over here, this way, follow me, and hundreds more. Turn until her voice is centred, walk to her, and when you are within two steps of her a chime sounds; after half a second of quiet she calls again from somewhere new. Five finds complete a level, and on each level she stands farther away.
  • A creature that listens. A blind creature shares the room and hunts by sound. The floor is concrete with patches of broken glass: step on glass and it charges, growling, with a footstep for every step it runs, until about a second after you are back on concrete. It runs a little slower than you can run. If it catches you, you lose a life.
  • Three lives and no clock. Your score is the number of levels you complete, on a high score board of its own, also shown on Game Stats. The first level is always the witch's, and every level after it has a different event background.
  • Her voice is Heart, a natural voice made by Kokoro on your own device, heard from where she stands. Until it is ready, or if your device cannot make it, your device's own voice says her words and a soft shimmering beacon marks where she stands. Her calls are always heard, even with the Speech setting turned Off.
  • The mode turns with you, as the other walking modes do. Its help section explains everything, with demos of its sounds.
  • The main menu's order: Find the Voice comes after Event High Scores, then Ultimate Mode, and Chaos Mode is now the last mode on the menu. The help's sections and the High Scores list follow the same order.

New: Bella, the game's voice during a run

Changed: Ultimate Mode

  • The world turns with you. A swipe left or right is now a turn, not a step: a quick turn to the next main direction, north, east, south or west, swept round in a quarter of a second, so two swipes turn you right round, and if you had stopped in between, a swipe squares you up again. A swipe is a quick turn however fast or slowly you make it, even if your finger pauses for an instant before it lifts. Slide left or right and hold, and you turn freely for as long as you hold: slowly at first, so a short hold is a small nudge for aiming, then faster, up to a full circle in about two and a half seconds. You stop exactly where you lift. Every sound around you sweeps round with the turn, the ones already sounding included; nothing in the world has moved, you have.
  • You walk where you face. A swipe up is one step the way you are facing and a swipe down is one step straight back, at any angle, so you can face a beacon by ear and walk straight to it. Running and walking backwards go the same way. Squarely into a wall you stop, as before. At a slant you slide along it, with the wall heard quietly beside you. Squared up with a quick turn, a labyrinth's corridors work exactly as they did.
  • A tick at each main direction. As you turn, a soft tick marks east, south and west as you pass them or land on them, and a higher tick marks north, so there is always one fixed point to map the world by. The game makes these sounds itself.
  • The aim is the quarter in front of you. A shot or a throw takes the nearest enemy within 45 degrees either side of straight ahead, so one a little to the side still counts, and nothing behind you; a chaser within two steps is still hit wherever it is. Before, a shot took whatever was roughly ahead of you or behind you. Quick turns alone are always enough to bring any enemy in front of you.
  • The compass turns you for real. With the compass on, your turns are your own turns: the beep rises as you face the way to go and falls as you turn away, and it beeps faster while you hold a turn. Each time you land on a main direction it says what lies ahead and where the way is: for example, This way, 6 steps, then left; or, Open for 4 steps, then a wall. The way is to your right. Turn the compass off and you are already facing the way you chose. Before, the compass had a pointer of its own and never turned you.
  • The report says the way you face. It now ends with one of eight words: for example, 100 health. 0 points. 10 shots. 3 items. Facing north-east. The line on the screen says it too.
  • On a computer, the arrows can be held. In Ultimate Mode the left and right arrows turn you, a press for a quick turn and a hold to turn freely, and the up and down arrows step forward and back. Held down, they run and walk backwards, which a keyboard could not do before, and you can turn while you run. A Braille notetaker, which cannot hold a key, makes one quick turn or one step with each press.
  • Smaller things. You step out of an island's elevator with your back to it, so walking on takes you away from it. Stepping anywhere onto the elevator's square starts the ride. A new world leaves you facing as you were, and a new run begins facing north. Gesture Practice names the new slide and hold, and the help topic on sounds behind you says that Ultimate Mode is now the exception to "north is always ahead". And the first time you start Ultimate Mode after this update, the game says once, in a few sentences, that turning has changed; nothing can hurt you while it does, and it is never said again on that device.
