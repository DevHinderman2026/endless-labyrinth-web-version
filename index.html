Endless Labyrinth

A 3D audio game, played by ear with headphones and touch gestures.

This web version is made for phones and tablets: an iPhone, an iPad, or an Android phone or tablet. On a computer, please play the Windows version, from the Releases page of the game's GitHub repository.

Find your way out of a labyrinth by ear before the clock runs out, or fight endless waves of enemies you can only hear. Everything you need is in the sound: where things are, how far away, and whether they are in front of you or behind you.

This is the web version of the Windows game, with its two first modes, Standard and Battle, for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Before you play

  • Wear headphones. Left and right matter.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, turn silent mode off. Silent mode mutes the game's sounds, though not its voice.
  • The game speaks for itself and listens to the whole screen. After the sounds load, it asks you to turn VoiceOver or TalkBack off while you play, and to turn it back on when you leave.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, add the game to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and play from there, so your high scores are kept safe.
  • The first start downloads the game's sounds, about 16 megabytes. After that they stay on your device.
