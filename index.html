Endless Labyrinth

A 3D audio game, played by ear with headphones: with touch gestures on a phone or tablet, or the keyboard on a computer.

You are on a Windows computer. From web version 5.0.0 the whole game plays here on Windows too, with the keyboard: every mode, the events and Ultimate Mode, in binaural 3D sound. Endless Labyrinth also has a Windows version to download, which works offline and has its own room echo on the voice, from the Releases page of the game's GitHub repository.

The complete game, now in your browser

Endless Labyrinth began as a game for Windows. Since November 2026 the complete game plays in the web browser of an iPhone, an iPad, an Android phone or tablet, a Chromebook, a Mac, a Linux computer or a Windows computer: all six modes and the same events as the Windows version, and nothing to install. Web version 2.0.0 added a seventh mode made for the web alone, Ultimate Mode: one huge, mixed world that regenerates at random, without warning, while you survive in it. Since version 5.0.0 the whole web game is heard in binaural 3D, and its walking modes turn with you: in Standard, Battle, Tower, Island, Chaos's walking levels and Ultimate Mode you turn where you stand and walk the way you face, while Driving and the events keep north ahead. The Windows version and this web version are developed side by side, and compatibility between them is maintained to the highest extent that both can allow. See the Update History for what each version brought.

Supported devices

Phones and tablets, played with touch gestures: an iPhone or iPad (iOS 18.4 or newer) and Android phones and tablets (with an up-to-date Chrome).

Computers, played with the keyboard: a Chromebook, a Mac or a Linux computer. Chrome, Edge or Safari is recommended; Firefox should work, but the game is not tested with it.

Windows computers play the whole game here too (from web version 5.0.0), with the keyboard, and with a Braille display and Braille keyboard through NVDA or JAWS. Endless Labyrinth also has a full Windows version to download, which works offline, with Braille display support and its own room echo on the voice: the Windows version.

The modes

Everything you need is in the sound: where things are, how far away they are, and whether they are in front of you or behind you.

  • Standard: find the exit of each labyrinth before the clock runs out.
  • Battle: hold off endless waves of enemies in an open arena.
  • Driving: drive a three-lane road, dodging or shooting oncoming cars.
  • Tower: climb a tower floor by floor, competing with a rival for the items on each floor.
  • Island: clear a chain of islands, diving to the seabed for each island's key before your air runs out.
  • Chaos: every kind of level, chosen at random, against a single five-minute clock.
  • Ultimate: the hardest mode, web only: survive in one huge world of labyrinths, islands, tower and battle areas and event places, in binaural 3D, that regenerates at random, without warning.
  • Events: limited-time challenges, shared with the Windows version.

Before you play

  • Wear headphones. Left and right matter.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, turn silent mode off. Silent mode mutes the game's sounds, though not its voice.
  • The game speaks for itself. After the sounds load, it asks you to turn your screen reader off while you play (VoiceOver, TalkBack, ChromeVox or Orca; on Windows, NVDA's or JAWS's speech), and to turn it back on when you leave.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, add the game to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and play from there, so your high scores are kept safe.
  • The first start downloads the game's sounds, about 120 megabytes, so use Wi-Fi if you can. After that they stay on your device, and only a sound pack that has changed is ever downloaded again.
