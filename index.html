A milestone: the whole game, now on your phone

Endless Labyrinth began as a game for Windows computers. As of November 2026, the whole game is fully playable in the web browser of an iPhone, an iPad or an Android phone or tablet: all six modes, Standard, Battle, Driving, Tower, Island and Chaos, and the same events as the Windows version. Nothing to install, and the same rules, timings and sounds as on Windows.

This is a huge step for the game: not just a Windows game any more, but one you can play by ear wherever your phone goes.

Phones and tablets only

This web version works only on an iPhone or iPad (iOS 18.4 or newer) and on Android phones and tablets (with an up-to-date Chrome).

Computers are not supported, including Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebooks, even with a touch screen. Endless Labyrinth already has a full Windows version for computers, with Braille display support and its own room echo on the voice, so the web version is made for touch devices.

Everything you need is in the sound: where things are, how far away, and whether they are in front of you or behind you. This is the web version of the Windows game, with every mode:

  • Standard: find the exit of each labyrinth before the clock runs out.
  • Battle: fight endless waves of enemies you can only hear.
  • Driving: race down a three-lane road, dodging or shooting the traffic.
  • Tower: climb a tower floor by floor, racing a rival for every item.
  • Island: clear each island, dive to the seabed for its key, and watch your air.
  • Chaos: every mode at once, one random level after another, against a five minute clock.
  • Events: time-limited challenges, the same ones the Windows version gets.

Before you play

  • Wear headphones. Left and right matter.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, turn silent mode off. Silent mode mutes the game's sounds, though not its voice.
  • The game speaks for itself and listens to the whole screen. After the sounds load, it asks you to turn VoiceOver or TalkBack off while you play, and to turn it back on when you leave.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, add the game to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and play from there, so your high scores are kept safe.
  • The first start downloads the game's sounds, about 120 megabytes, so use Wi-Fi if you can. After that they stay on your device, and only a sound pack that has changed is ever downloaded again.
