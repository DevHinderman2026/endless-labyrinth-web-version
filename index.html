Endless Labyrinth

A 3D audio game, played by ear with headphones: with touch gestures on a phone or tablet, or the keyboard on a computer.

You are on a Windows computer. From web version 5.0.0 the whole game plays here on Windows too, with the keyboard: every mode, the events and Ultimate Mode, in binaural 3D sound. Endless Labyrinth also has a Windows version to download, which works offline, from the Releases page of the game's GitHub repository.

The complete game, now in your browser

Endless Labyrinth began as a game for Windows. Since November 2026 the complete game plays in the web browser of an iPhone, an iPad, an Android phone or tablet, a Chromebook, a Mac, a Linux computer or a Windows computer: every mode and the same events as the Windows version, and nothing to install. The website also has modes of its own: Ultimate Mode (web version 2.0.0), one huge, mixed world that regenerates at random, without warning, while you survive in it; Find the Voice (6.0.0); The Abyss (6.2.0); Water Park (7.0.0); and The Museum (7.2.0). Since version 5.0.0 the whole web game is heard in binaural 3D, and its walking modes turn with you: in Standard, Battle, Tower, Island, Find the Voice, The Abyss, Water Park, The Museum, Chaos's walking levels and Ultimate Mode you turn where you stand and walk the way you face, while Driving and the events keep north ahead. Since version 6.0.0 the game also has a voice of its own, Bella, during a run. The Windows version and this web version are developed side by side, and compatibility between them is maintained to the highest extent that both can allow. See the Update History for what each version brought.

Supported devices

Phones and tablets, played with touch gestures: an iPhone or iPad (iOS 18.4 or newer) and Android phones and tablets (with an up-to-date Chrome).

Computers, played with the keyboard: a Chromebook, a Mac or a Linux computer. Chrome, Edge or Safari is recommended; Firefox should work, but the game is not tested with it.

Windows computers play the whole game here too (from web version 5.0.0), with the keyboard, and with a Braille display and Braille keyboard through NVDA or JAWS. Endless Labyrinth also has a full Windows version to download, which works offline, with Braille display support: the Windows version.

The modes

Everything you need is in the sound: where things are, how far away they are, and whether they are in front of you or behind you.

  • Standard: find the exit of each labyrinth before the clock runs out.
  • Battle: hold off endless waves of enemies in an open arena.
  • Driving: drive a three-lane road, dodging or shooting oncoming cars.
  • Tower: climb a tower floor by floor, competing with a rival for the items on each floor.
  • Island: clear a chain of islands, diving to the seabed for each island's key before your air runs out.
  • Find the Voice: find a calling voice by ear, again and again, while a blind creature listens for your steps on broken glass.
  • The Abyss: web only: an endless abyss floored with broken glass, where blind creatures hunt you only while you move; smash glass cases for time under cover of the lightning.
  • Water Park: web only: win a token at every attraction of a water park before closing time, the water slide, the diving pool, the wave pool, the tipping bucket and the duck stand, each found by its own sound, and every park bigger than the last.
  • The Museum: web only: free a character's soul by finding every memory in their museum, guided by their voice, past bottomless pools and sleeping creatures, then walk out of the door into the next museum.
  • Chaos: every kind of level, chosen at random, against a single five-minute clock.
  • Ultimate: the hardest mode, web only: survive in one huge world of labyrinths, islands, tower and battle areas and event places, in binaural 3D, that regenerates at random, without warning.
  • Events: limited-time challenges, shared with the Windows version.

Before you play

  • Wear headphones. Left and right matter.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, turn silent mode off. Silent mode mutes the game's sounds, though not its voice.
  • The game speaks for itself: in the menus with your device's own voice, and during a run with its own voice, Bella. After the sounds load, it asks you to turn your screen reader off while you play (VoiceOver, TalkBack, ChromeVox or Orca; on Windows, NVDA's or JAWS's speech), and to turn it back on when you leave.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, add the game to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and play from there, so your high scores are kept safe.
  • The first start downloads the game's sounds, about 120 megabytes, and its voices, about 95 megabytes more, so use Wi-Fi if you can. After that they stay on your device, and only a sound pack that has changed is ever downloaded again.
  • The static some players heard on an iPhone or iPad has had two causes fixed, in versions 7.0.0 and 7.1.5. If you ever still hear static or crackling, pause the game and resume it, and it goes away. See the frequently asked questions.
