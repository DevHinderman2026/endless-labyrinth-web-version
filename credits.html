The game

Endless Labyrinth is designed and directed by Russell Hinderman (DevHinderman2026).

AI assisted. The game's code was written with the help of AI: Claude, by Anthropic, working under Russell's direction. Russell decides how every part of the game works, sounds and plays, and tests it by ear; the AI writes and checks the code that carries those decisions out.

Sounds

Every recorded sound in Endless Labyrinth comes from Pixabay and is used under the Pixabay Content License. Each recording has been edited for the game and travels only inside the game's sound packs; please do not take them out of the game to share them on their own, and get them from Pixabay instead. Every other sound, including all beacons, the enemies, warnings and the menus, is made by the game itself.
